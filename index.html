I’m Saurabh Ranjan, a cognitive neuroscientist who builds bridges between human cognition and modern AI systems. My dissertation, Reality Monitoring in Humans and Artificial Intelligence, showed that humans and LLMs share a core capability that fails during hallucination — combining behavioral experiments, neuroimaging (UK Biobank, MEG/EEG), and matched human–LLM benchmarking pipelines.

I'm currently a Researcher in AI for Biomedical Health Outcomes at UF, co-developing UF's Master's curriculum on Data Science and Agentic LLMs.

I previously worked with Dr. Brian Odegaard (blue sky, twitter\x) at PACLAB, and with Prof. Narayanan Srinivasan (now at IIT Kanpur) at CBCS.

My publications are on Google Scholar, and my open-source work (agentic LLM tooling, cognitive modeling) is on GitHub.

Highlighted Projects

Mind & Cognition

🪞

Reality Monitoring with Humans and LLMs

  • Discovered that humans and LLMs share a core "reality monitoring" capability that fails during hallucination.
  • Designed and ran behavioral experiments with 100+ human participants, matched against LLM simulations.
  • Built standardized human–LLM benchmarking pipelines across conversation-history and feedback conditions.
  • Found a dissociation between single-trial and conversation-memory regimes in LLMs that inverts source-attribution accuracy.
  • Findings imply how clinical AI assistants should manage chart history and cited evidence.

GitHub: LLM-RM · GitHub: predict_perception_imagination_tgm · Papers

🎨

Human Imagination

  • Proposed a new computational model of visual imagination bridging heterarchical and hierarchical theories of the brain.
  • Built imagination networks compared across Florida, London, and Poland cohorts.
  • Used psychological network analysis to compare population-level cognitive structure between humans and synthesized LLM populations.
  • Framed offline "world models" as the AI analogue of human imagination networks.
  • Methodology generalizes to subgroup discovery in health and behavioral data.

GitHub: imagine_world_model · Preprint (arXiv)

Education and Cognition

  • Studied embodied-interaction technology design to support model-based reasoning in developing-country classrooms.
  • Co-designed "touchy-feely" digital affordances that improved students' epistemic access to abstract science concepts.
  • Investigated how undergraduate students reason about ethical and algorithmic decision-making.
  • Produced behavioral analyses for an embodied-cognition educational technology intervention at TIFR.
  • Published at IEEE T4E, ACM SIGCSE, and in the Journal of Computer Assisted Learning.

  • Automates and scales "LLMs as a participant" studies.
